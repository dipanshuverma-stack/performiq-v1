# AI Strategy

## Philosophy

Use AI only when required.

Everything else should be rule-based.

## No Gemini Required

- Dashboard
- Analytics
- Readiness Score
- Revision Engine
- Subject Intelligence
- Progress Tracking

## Gemini Features

### Weak Area Analysis

Cooldown:
12 Hours

### Adaptive Study Plan

Cooldown:
12 Hours

### Motivational Coach

Cooldown:
6 Hours

### Achievement Summary

Cooldown:
12 Hours

## Cache Strategy

Generate once.

Store in database.

Reuse until expiry.

Do not regenerate automatically.

## Cost Goal

Normal usage:
0 AI calls

Power usage:
1-3 AI calls per day
